# Curriculum scope

California Kindergarten and Grade 1 were researched and mapped first. Grades 2 through 12 use **starter editorial topic shelves** suitable for catalogue navigation. Topic counts are not counts of verified standards; there is no claim of a complete or state-approved curriculum. Science, health, PE, history and older-grade course/grade matches need educator review before publication.

The canonical editable topic identifiers and sources are in `data/curriculum.json`. Avoid fabricating grade-specific state standards and preserve the K–2 band distinction for computing where it is applicable.
